# Datasets: Modeling Extreme Events in the Presence of Inliers: A Mixture Approach

This repository contains the two real-world datasets used in the paper **“Modeling Extreme Events in the Presence of Inliers: A Mixture Approach”**, published in the *Journal of Applied Statistics* (2025).

## Datasets

### Dataset 1: **Earnings Dataset:** `cps91` earnings data from the `wooldridge` package in R.

### Dataset 2: **Modikuppam Rainfall Dataset:** Monthly rainfall observations from Modikuppam, Tamil Nadu, India.

## Reference

Nila, S., Das, I., & Balakrishna, N. (2025). Modeling extreme events in the presence of inliers: a mixture approach. *Journal of Applied Statistics*.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1080/02664763.2025.2606244
